# Hartsfield Business Bank — Brand Bible
*Consolidated from /references (Backstory, Brand Voice, Approved Claims, Current Campaign Messaging, Product Sheet, Compliance) into a single audit-ready rubric. This is the document the brand-auditor agent checks every ad angle against. Source PDFs remain in /references as the primary record — this file is the working synthesis, not a replacement.*

## 1. Who Hartsfield Is
Founded 1999 by Calvin Reeves (former SunTrust commercial loan officer) after his mother was denied a business line of credit for having "seasonal revenue." Built on a loan process that weighs owner character and community ties alongside credit scores. Now: $2.1B in assets, 9 metro Atlanta branches + 2 in Savannah, for a community bank in Georgia. Launching **Hartsfield Momentum Business Suite** — the bank's first fully digital product — without a branch visit required, while keeping human advisors.

## 2. Brand Personality
Ambitious, modern, rooted. A mentor who's ahead of the curve and remembers where you started. Not corporate, not cold, never performative about community — genuinely of it.

## 3. Voice Pillars
- **Straight talk.** No jargon, no fine print buried in enthusiasm. If a sentence needs a footnote to be honest, rewrite the sentence.
- **Earned confidence.** Declarative, not defensive — 25 years of "yes" earns the right to speak plainly. Doesn't need to convince anyone it belongs.
- **Forward motion.** Every line points at something — a goal, a milestone, a next step. Not inspirational fluff.
- **Warmth without softness.** Care shows up as clarity, speed, and honesty — not just smiling language.

## 4. Tone by Channel
| Channel | Tone |
|---|---|
| Website headlines | Declarative, forward-moving, no fluff |
| Email | Warm, direct, one clear action per message |
| Social media | Conversational, Atlanta-specific, celebrates customers more than the brand |
| Product copy | Plain language, specific numbers, zero ambiguity |
| Legal/compliance notices | Clear and complete — never hidden, never minimized |

**Sounds like:** "Your business doesn't fit in a box. Neither does your banking." / "Built in Atlanta. Built for what's next." / "We've been saying yes since 1999. That hasn't changed."

**Never sounds like:** "Synergistic financial solutions for the modern entrepreneur." / "We're more than a bank — we're a partner in your journey." / "Empowering small businesses to reach their full potential." / Anything a committee outside Atlanta could have written.

## 5. Current Campaign (baseline being evolved)
- Tagline: "Banking That Knows Your Name."
- Pillars: Relationship (named relationship manager) → Local Decision Making (underwritten in Atlanta, not Charlotte/Dallas) → Community Track Record (25 years, stayed open in 2008, expedited COVID loans).
- **Known weakness (internal assessment):** over-indexes on heritage/relationship, says nothing about digital tools, account features, or rates. Reads as warm but analog against a national competitor launching a no-fee digital suite with AI cash flow tools. New angles must carry the relationship equity forward into a modern product story without abandoning the trust already built.

## 6. Momentum Business Suite — What's Actually In It
- **Checking:** no monthly fee, unlimited fee-free transactions, free ACH + incoming wires, spend-control debit cards, mobile deposit, up to $5M FDIC coverage via IntraFi sweep.
- **Savings:** 3.75% APY (variable — must always be disclosed as such and dated), no minimum balance, no monthly fee, 6 fee-free withdrawals/month, auto-sweep rules.
- **Momentum Intelligence Dashboard:** 90-day cash flow forecast from real transaction data, auto-categorization, invoicing + payment status alerts, payroll scheduling integration (QuickBooks, Gusto, ADP), tax reserve calculator, anomaly alerts.
- **Momentum Connect:** QuickBooks/Xero, Shopify/Square/Stripe, Gusto/ADP, HubSpot.
- **Momentum Human Layer:** named Atlanta-based advisor, phone/video/in-branch — no bots.
- **Pricing:** Starter $0/mo, Pro $49/mo (+ Connect + dedicated advisor), Enterprise custom.
- **Differentiator to use carefully:** first cash flow intelligence tool built by a Georgia-chartered community bank — the claim is *institutional knowledge trained on local market patterns*, not raw feature parity. Comparisons must stay factual (see §8) — don't imply national players/fintechs lack any cash flow tools at all, only that none combine it with 25 years of local underwriting.

## 7. Approved Claims (cleared for all channels, as-is)
- "Locally owned and operated since 1999"
- "Nine metro Atlanta locations plus two in Savannah"
- "FDIC insured up to $250,000 per depositor" (full phrase only — never shortened)
- "Loan decisions made locally — no out-of-state underwriting"
- "Momentum accounts include no monthly maintenance fees" (must carry current rate disclosure alongside)
- "Up to [X]% APY on Momentum Business Savings" (must be current + labeled variable)
- "Integrated tools including cash flow forecasting, invoicing, and expense tracking"
- "Human support available by phone, in-branch, or video — no bots"

## 8. Requires Legal Review Before Use
- Any specific APY figure (rates are variable; must reflect current published rate)
- Any comparison naming a competitor ("unlike Mercury" or similar)
- Any claim implying guaranteed returns/performance
- "Best" without a cited, dated, third-party source
- Any loan-approval-speed claim not backed by documented average processing times

## 9. Never Permitted — Hard Bans
- "Guaranteed" for any rate, return, or loan outcome
- "Risk-free" / "no risk" / "zero risk" for any financial product
- "Government-backed" unless specifically SBA loan products
- "FDIC insured up to $250,000" without the full disclosure attached
- "Insured by the government" (must say FDIC specifically)
- "Safe as a government bond" or similar comparative-safety claims
- "Guaranteed rate" / "locked rate" (unless a CD with a stated term)
- "Unlimited FDIC coverage" (coverage is per depositor, per ownership category — has limits)
- Any implication Momentum accounts are securities/investment products
- Any testimonial without signed written consent on file, or from a composite/fabricated customer

## 10. Compliance Mechanics (not just word bans — placement rules)
- **FDIC disclosure** must appear in every piece referencing deposit accounts — "Member FDIC" standalone label or full statement in body copy. Applies to social posts too.
- **APY disclosure** — current rate + "as of [date]" + "variable, subject to change" + minimum balance if applicable, must appear **in the same unit of communication** as the rate (not a linked footnote, not off-screen). A headline stating a rate without this alongside it is non-compliant, not just risky.
- **Email/CAN-SPAM:** physical mailing address, unsubscribe mechanism, accurate sender ID, no false-urgency subject lines (e.g., "Your account has been flagged" is explicitly banned as a hook).
- **Testimonials (FTC):** must reflect typical results or disclose that results aren't typical; signed release required before publication.
- **Social media:** "DM us for details" does not satisfy disclosure requirements — the claim and its disclosure must be in the same post.

## 11. Do / Don't (illustrative, drawn from the rules above)
| Don't | Do | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "Earn up to 4% on your business savings." | "3.75% APY on Momentum Business Savings, variable, as of [date]. No minimum balance." | Rate stated without required disclosure in the same unit — non-compliant per §10. |
| "Unlike Mercury, we actually know Atlanta." | *(hold — route to legal review)* | Named-competitor comparison requires legal review per §8, even if factually true. |
| "Guaranteed to grow your savings faster." | "3.75% variable APY — see current rate and terms." | "Guaranteed" is a hard ban per §9. |
| "FDIC insured." | "Member FDIC. Deposits insured up to $250,000 per depositor." | Shortened FDIC claim is explicitly banned per §9 — full phrase required. |
